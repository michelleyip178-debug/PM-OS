## Opportunities Visibility for Officers Holding Two Jobs — Decision Needed from PSD

**Date:** 2026-09-17

**To:** [BO names — confirm distribution]

**From:** Michelle Yip

**Ask:** 1 policy decision on how CareerCompass decides what an officer can see, when that officer holds two jobs at once.

---

### The Situation

Some officers hold two active jobs at the same time — either because they're "double-hatting" (covering two roles) or on secondment (posted to a second agency while still attached to their home agency). CareerCompass needs a rule for what these officers are allowed to see in the Opportunities catalog, since some postings are restricted to specific agencies or departments.

Most cases are simple: if a posting is open to Agency A, and the officer works at Agency A (even just one of their two jobs), they see it. No disagreement there.

The one case that needs a decision: what happens when a posting is restricted in a way that touches **both** of the officer's jobs at once, but neither job on its own would qualify them.

### A Concrete Example

An officer works two jobs: Finance at BCA, and Ops at LTA.

A gig is closed to BCA Finance officers, and separately closed to LTA Ops officers — in other words, it's designed to keep out exactly the two kinds of people this officer is, one from each of their two jobs.

**Should this officer see the gig?**

### The Two Options

| | Option A: Check each job on its own (Recommended) | Option B: Combine facts across both jobs |
|---|---|---|
| **In plain terms** | Look at each of the officer's jobs individually, the same way a person doing this manually would. They qualify if at least one whole job would get them in. | Take everything true about the officer across both jobs, mix it together, and check each piece separately. |
| **In the example above** | They don't see the gig. Neither their BCA-Finance job nor their LTA-Ops job qualifies on its own. | They do see the gig. Taken separately, "has an LTA job" and "has a Finance job" each look fine on their own — even though neither job of theirs is actually LTA-and-Finance together. |
| **The risk** | An officer might not see something they feel they should, because it's judged job-by-job | An officer can end up seeing something based on a mash-up of facts that doesn't match any job they actually hold — effectively bypassing a restriction the posting owner intended |

### Our Recommendation

**Option A** — check each job on its own. It matches how anyone reviewing this by hand would think about it ("does this specific job of yours qualify you"), and it doesn't let an officer through on a combination of facts that isn't true of any single job they hold. Option B creates a real gap: an agency restricting a posting to keep out a specific group could end up admitting people anyway, purely because they hold an unrelated second job elsewhere.

### What We Need From You

Confirm Option A as the standing rule for how CareerCompass handles officers with two active jobs. This decision applies across all current and future cases of double-hatting and secondment, so it only needs to be made once.
