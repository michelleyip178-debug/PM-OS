### What R1 Delivers for PSD

- **Covers the mobility core OTG never could.** Rotations and Secondments made up 51% of all government mobility postings in 2025 (587 of 1,035). OTG can't accept a resume upload for any of them — CareerCompass will.
- **Ends candidate silence.** A simple status tracker (Submitted → Under Review → Outcome) with automatic 30-day closure recovers an estimated 89 placements a year that currently go unrecorded.
- **Moves us off OTG faster.** A unified catalog retires 40% of what OTG currently does in this release, on the path to full shutdown by 2028.

### What CareerCompass Will Not Do

It will not become a full recruitment system — no interview scheduling, no candidate scoring, no custom application forms per agency. Selection stays offline or on agencies' own tools. This keeps the release scoped and deliverable on the committed timeline.
