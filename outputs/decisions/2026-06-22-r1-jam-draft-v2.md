## R1 in one line

MVP gets an officer to the *door* of an opportunity — browse, filter, view, click out to apply. **R1 brings both ends in-house:** agencies create opportunities directly in CareerCompass, officers apply without leaving it. Create it, post it, pre-fill it, submit it, track it, save it. CareerCompass stops being a read-only window onto OTG and becomes the system of record.

## The 4 epics

### Epic A — Opportunity Creation
Agencies author and manage postings directly in CareerCompass: a structured creation form, the posting lives in OTEP's DB, validation + publish workflow, and the edit/close lifecycle.

**Recommended scope — narrow, not full:**
Lead with **Internal Jobs + Secondments only**. These are the two types with no other home post-OTG (pulled from MVP because there's no creation tooling). Full 5-type creation (adding STIP, Gig, PSFG) is the stretch, not the base.

**Why this is the spine:** it flips CareerCompass from read-only into the system of record. It's also the prerequisite that lets Internal Jobs + Secondments back in.

**Scope flags for the jam:**
- Narrow (IJ+Secondment) = ~half the build vs full 5-type. This is the capacity lever.
- **Criteria authoring: recommend OUT of R1.** Keeps R1 off the blocked POCDEX write path (#31). Criteria stays OTG-read for R1; authoring comes R1.5.
- **SJR: NOT in R1 creation** unless Mark explicitly pulls it in.
- **C@G: stays on its own rails**, not the generic create form.
- Agency-admin auth is an **open dependency** — who they are and how they authenticate is currently undefined. (→ Pow Hwee / Fabian)

---

### Epic B — Streamlined Application + Smart Pre-fill
Two things bundled together: the native in-Compass apply form, and intelligent pre-population from the officer's profile.

**Apply core:**
A full in-Compass application form — native OTEP form, not a FormSG/C@G embed. Compass owns the form, pre-filled from the officer's OTEP profile (competencies + work history), posting to OTEP's own backend. Officer completes and submits without leaving Compass. No redirects.

**Smart pre-fill (folded in from original Epic E):**
Auto-populates the officer's strengths and experience fields on the form, drawn from their existing OTEP profile. This is **profile-driven pre-fill only** — not CV-upload inference (that's OTEP-205, a separate capability). The pre-fill is a sub-feature of the form, not a standalone epic.

**Design note:** The pre-filled-then-editable form needs intentional design even if the smart inference is cut. What does the officer see when pre-fill is wrong, partial, or stale? That interaction lives in B regardless of how much "smart" we add. Don't let the inference question leave the core form interaction undesigned.

**Cross-squad dependency (flag):**
Profile pre-fill rides on the competency SSOT owned by Imelda's squad — endpoint specs still in flight between Léo and Kingsley (#18/#41). Pre-fill is not a given until that contract is finalised.

**Open questions:** profile-field → form-field mapping (→ Amber / Pow Hwee)

---

### Epic C — Status Tracking
End-to-end application monitoring, native in OTEP. OTEP owns the state machine: submitted → under review → outcome. **No ATS integration in R1** — that's R2 or whenever a vendor is confirmed.

**Depends on:** Epic B (need a submitted application to have a status).

**Design load:** The "outcome = rejection" state and "no applications yet" empty state are real design surfaces, not just schema. These are Amber's and currently unnamed. (→ Amber + Pow Hwee)

**Open questions:** what are the states? Who triggers transitions? Who sees the status — officer only, or HR too?

---

### Epic D — Saved Jobs
Officers save/bookmark opportunities and resume in-progress applications without losing data.

**Two halves:**
- "Save an opportunity" — lighter, can ship semi-independently
- "Resume an application" — waits on Epic B (needs a form to resume)

**Open questions:** cross-session persistence? What happens when a saved posting closes? (→ Pow Hwee)

## Priority order

| # | Epic | Why here | Confidence |
|---|------|----------|------------|
| 1 | **A — Opportunity Creation (narrow: IJ + Secondment)** | Upstream spine. Biggest build at full scope — narrow-first halves the risk. IJ+Secondment have no other home. | High (narrow) / Low (full) |
| 2 | **B — Streamlined Application + Smart Pre-fill** | Officer-facing payoff. The native form everything else hangs off. Smart pre-fill folded in as sub-feature — don't treat it as a separate epic. | High |
| 3 | **C — Status Tracking** | Closes the "submit into a black box" problem. New data model = scope early even if it ships after B. | High |
| 4 | **D — Saved Jobs** | High officer value, lower build cost. "Save an opportunity" can ship semi-independently as a parallel track. | Medium |

**The critical path:** A and B are the two spines (creation + application). Different surfaces — scoping parallelises, but **build serialises through one FE (Thomas)**. C is scoped alongside B, ships after. D is the parallel-track candidate.

**If R1 has to cut:** D's "resume application" half goes first, then D entirely. A-narrow + B + C is the non-negotiable core — without all three, R1 doesn't deliver its promise.

**Fallback shape (have this ready):** if A-narrow + B + C doesn't fit, the minimum is **A-narrow + B, with C as email/manual interim (slips to R1.5).** Still closes the create→apply loop for the orphaned types.
