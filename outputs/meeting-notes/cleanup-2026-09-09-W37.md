## Meeting 1: Architecture & Scalability Review (~1h20m)

**Attendees:** Thomas Huchede, Rathika Ramalingam, Léo Milbor, Victor (CIE), Michelle Yip, infra/platform

**Summary:**

- Role recommendation and search do too much in the app tier and over-fetch from the database; OOM and latency risk under load. Phased fix agreed: correctness first, move logic to SQL as it stabilises.
- Near-term fixes locked: optimise two heavy indexes, remove a UID-to-text cast blocking an index, enforce a 3-char minimum on full search.
- Biggest gaps: no shared latency/concurrency SLO, CIE monitoring is bare bones, failed opportunity imports silently delete the previous record.

**Decisions:**

1. Keep the phased app-tier-first approach for role recommendation and search.
2. Proceed with the two index optimisations and remove the UID text cast.
3. Enforce a 3-char minimum on full search (Michelle takes the UX side to product).
4. Compass must degrade gracefully when POCDEX is unavailable (onboarding stops, profile refresh delays, existing users continue on stale data).
5. Move more filtering and pagination into SQL where it stays maintainable.
6. Playwright stays the E2E standard; Rider stays the ingestion backbone; expose job status via Rider's API.
7. Re-ranking fallback (semantic top-N if Bedrock is slow): direction agreed, not finalised.
8. A dedicated ML training environment is needed for future recommenders.
