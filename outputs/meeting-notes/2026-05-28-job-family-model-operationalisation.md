## Summary

The group worked through how to operationalise job family changes across agencies — roles, timelines, and comms. Ownership is settled: WD leads the policy narrative, system teams own implementation guidance. Key dates are anchored: changes effective 31 Dec 2026, competency re-tagging for 2027 appraisal cycle, deactivation Q1 2027. But timelines are aspirational until system teams return effort estimates — that sizing work is the critical unlocker for everything downstream.

---

## Decisions Made

### 1. WD leads policy; system teams own implementation and comms input

- **Decision:** WD owns the policy intent and narrative. System teams contribute system implications and user instructions for agency comms.
- **Rationale:** Clean separation of responsibilities — WD knows what's changing and why; system teams know how it plays out in HRP systems.
- **Impact:** All comms go out under WD's voice, with system teams having reviewed for accuracy and added system-side guidance.

### 2. Timeline anchored: retag 2H 2026, deactivate Q1 2027

- **Decision:** Job family changes to be effected by 31 Dec 2026. Competency re-tagging applies to the 2027 appraisal cycle, not 2026. Outdated job families and competencies deactivated Q1 2027.
- **Rationale:** Avoids disrupting the current appraisal cycle. Gives agencies 2H 2026 to action changes before frameworks are retired.
- **Impact:** All system estimates and agency comms must be sequenced against this window. Timeline is at risk if effort sizing comes back heavy — no buffer has been discussed.
- **Caveat:** System teams raised feasibility concerns on the Sep–Dec window. Not resolved.

### 3. No immediate deactivation of agency self-upload

- **Decision:** No decision to deactivate agency self-upload functionality in HRP systems now. Current state continues.
- **Rationale:** Deferred pending ComBank rationalisation and Phase 2 review. Broader governance question (process vs system control) still being debated.
- **Future:** Deactivation may happen post-rationalisation — flagged as needing deeper discussion, not a current action.

### 4. Agency comms: high-level at July HRM, detailed separately

- **Decision:** High-level job family changes communicated to agencies at July HRM. Detailed per-agency impacts go out separately.
- **Rationale:** July HRM is the natural broadcast forum. Detailed comms require system-team input and per-agency analysis — not ready for July.
- **Open:** Whether detailed comms are per-agency or consolidated hasn't been agreed.

### 5. Agencies guided to check with PSD before uploading agency-specific FCs

- **Decision:** Process intent is clear — agencies should check with WD / system teams before uploading to avoid duplicate functional competencies.
- **Caveat:** No finalised workflow, no SLA, no comms approach. This is intent without a mechanism.

## Key Context

**Why effort sizing is the critical path:**
Every timeline in this meeting — July HRM comms, Dec 31 changes, Q1 2027 deactivation — depends on system teams confirming what's actually buildable in that window. Until HRPS and Cumulus return estimates, the plan is aspirational. The first forcing function is the WD confirmed change list in early July, which needs sizing to exist before it can be actioned.

**Retagging assumption mismatch:**
WD's stated design principle is to minimise retagging ("lift and shift where possible"). System teams pushed back — retagging is still required in practice. This tension wasn't resolved and it matters: if the "lift and shift" assumption is wrong, agency workload is higher than what the comms will promise. Needs explicit resolution before any agency communication goes out.
