## Meeting 3: CareerCompass R1 Opportunities Scope Jamming (13:30-15:00)

**Attendees:** Michelle Yip (PM, PSD), Li Ting Kway (Design), Pow Hwee (Tech Lead input)

**Summary:**
- Integrated findings from the Gigs and SJR cross-program analysis: 89% of SJRs end with unrecorded outcomes, 90% of applicants churn after one cycle, and 1 single applicant submitted 112 applications in 2026 due to missing grade gates.
- Confirmed that there is zero policy requirement for an approving moderator in Gigs or SJRs, dropping unnecessary approval bottlenecks from product specs.
- Clarified the FormSG operational boundary: adopt WD's adaptable FormSG template for lightweight STIPs/Gigs, while building native CV upload, candidate rosters, and status tracking for substantive roles (SJRs, Internal Jobs, Secondments).
- Locked four core strategic trade-offs to deliver an end-to-end selection pipeline within a strict 5.5-sprint budget.

**Decisions:**
1. Lock R1 MVP scope boundary at 5.5 sprints across 8 RICE-ranked features. (Owner: Michelle Yip, Alignment: R1 Delivery)
2. Adopt a split application flow: WD FormSG template for STIPs/Gigs, and native CV upload with tracking for SJRs/Jobs/Secondments. (Owner: Michelle Yip, Alignment: Pragmatic Staging)
3. Drop central approving moderator gate; hiring agency HR and posting managers decide directly. (Owner: Michelle Yip, Alignment: Lean ATS)
4. Implement structured job grade tags (F-09) as the primary filter to curb serial spray-and-pray applications. (Owner: Michelle Yip, Alignment: Screening Efficiency)
5. Adopt a one-way outbound link model from OTG to Compass; reject complex bi-directional data synchronization. (Owner: Michelle Yip, Alignment: Technical Feasibility)
