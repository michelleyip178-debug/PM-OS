## Decisions Made

1. **Pursue incremental/interim NCS findings instead of an internal pre-scan.**
   - **Why:** A parallel pre-scan risks tool-finding mismatches, competes with the imminent code freeze for team bandwidth, and might not even align with NCS's actual findings — expensive activity for uncertain payoff.
   - **Who decided:** Team consensus, driven by Adrian Ang's timeline concern.
   - **Impact:** Puts the mitigation burden on NCS's reporting cadence rather than internal duplicate work — see Risk 1 below for why this isn't fully de-risked.

2. **NCS asked to release findings per assessment component, not as one final report.**
   - **Why:** Shrinking buffer between interim report and go-live means waiting for a single consolidated report is no longer viable.
   - **Who decided:** Rama Moorthy to action.
   - **Impact:** Becomes the single point of failure for the whole VAPT timeline recovery plan (see Risk 1).

3. **Product/change freeze confirmed: product changes freeze Wednesday, technical fixes allowed until Friday, future MVP changes need approval.**
   - **Why:** VAPT scanning needs a stable target; scope changes mid-scan invalidate findings.
   - **Who decided:** Rama Moorthy (announced).
   - **Impact:** Approval authority and exception process for "future MVP changes need approval" are still undefined — flagged as Risk 5.

4. **Identity architecture stays NRIC-based for now; "last modified date" reconfirmed as important; further POCDEX-enhancement asks wait for more BO discussion.**
   - **Why:** POCDEX UID could solve some continuity edge cases in theory, but not all agencies are onboarded to POCDEX, and some (MINDEF, DSTA) provide Malaysian ICs instead of NRICs — a POCDEX-only design would break for them.
   - **Who decided:** Rama Moorthy, Pow Hwee Tan, Adrian Ang, Michelle Yip (Michelle contributed OTG's existing non-POCDEX/Malaysian-IC handling experience).
   - **Impact:** This is a directional call, not a resolved one — the core question ("what constitutes the same officer?") is still open. Directly reinforces yesterday's Adrian-sync decision that "last modified date" is accepted as a working requirement (see [2026-08-31-W36-adrian-biweekly-sync.md](2026-08-31-W36-adrian-biweekly-sync.md)).

5. **CMM discovery: finish remaining agency interviews first; share findings with BOs on 15 September; keep developing an internal point of view before broader co-creation.**
   - **Why:** Discovery has matured from "what are the pains" to "what should the solution look like" — sequencing internal alignment before external co-creation avoids the team having to walk back infeasible ideas stakeholders generate live.
   - **Who decided:** Li Ting Kway and team, with Adrian Ang flagging the co-creation-guardrails risk.
   - **Impact:** Buys time to build a defensible POV, but the guardrails-for-co-creation approach itself isn't agreed yet (Risk 4).

## Identity Architecture — Deep Dive (added post-meeting, third pass)

A third AI-generated assessment focused specifically on the identity/NRIC decision and its downstream phase impact — merged in here since it's the same meeting, going deeper on one thread.

**Current decision (MVP):** proceed with NRIC as primary identifier — aligns with policy and existing integration patterns. POCDEX UID, FIN→NRIC transitions, and other special identity handling get evaluated later, if and when business scenarios justify them.

**Impact on future phases:**

| Area | Why it matters | MVP (Phase 1) | Future (Phase 2+) |
|------|-----------------|----------------|---------------------|
| **Non-POCDEX agency expansion** | OTG already runs separate pipelines for MINDEF, DSTA, A*STAR, CPF — agencies outside the POCDEX ecosystem. Malaysian IC identifiers exist in production. WD may onboard agencies OTG previously deprioritised; SingPass integration may become necessary if non-POCDEX onboarding expands. | No impact — NRIC approach lets delivery proceed | Additional onboarding work; identity-matching/auth design enhancement; new agency onboarding effort could grow |
| **Officer identity continuity** | Rama's core concern: officers changing identity types (FIN→NRIC) lose continuity under NRIC alone, where POCDEX's UID naturally preserves it. Career/learning/competency/recommendation history and longitudinal analytics all assume "this person today is the same person as before" — an assumption with known edge cases | Deferred | Potential profile-reconciliation capability; potential data migration effort; potential enhancement requests to upstream systems — classic technical debt, resurfaces once longitudinal career features matter |
| **Employment profile change handling** | Direction agreed: detect profile changes, refresh officer-related data. Covers last-modified-date, double-hatting, multiple employments, supervisory relationships | Lowest complexity, fastest delivery — detect + refresh | Understanding *what* changed and how it affects recommendations/competencies/pathways may need a smarter model than "refresh everything" if employment complexity grows |
| **Competency & Career Marketplace (CMM) vision** | Competency management, job profile management, workflow automation, HR-led maintenance — all person-centric capabilities that depend on a stable identity model. A fragmented identity model makes downstream features harder to implement reliably | No immediate impact | Potential future impact on discovery, design, data architecture, reporting/analytics |

**The real strategic risk (per this assessment):** the team framed the discussion as "NRIC vs. POCDEX UID" — but the more important question is *"What is Compass's long-term identity authority?"*

| Option | MVP Suitability | Long-term Suitability |
|--------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| NRIC only | ✅ High | ⚠️ Moderate |
| POCDEX UID only | ❌ Limited (not all agencies onboarded) | ⚠️ Moderate |
| Identity abstraction layer | ❌ Overkill for MVP | ✅ Strong |

Deferring the broader question is a reasonable product call — it keeps architecture debate from delaying VAPT and MVP delivery — but it's worth naming explicitly that the team chose NRIC-for-now over the eventual right answer (an abstraction layer), not because NRIC is the long-term fit.

**RAID log entries (per this assessment):**

- **Risk:** identity model may not fully support future onboarding of non-POCDEX agencies and identity-transition scenarios (e.g. FIN→NRIC), resulting in future rework.
- **Assumption:** NRIC-based identification is sufficient for MVP business processes and target onboarding agencies.
- **Decision:** proceed with NRIC as primary identifier; defer broader identity strategy until post-MVP validation and future onboarding requirements are clearer.
- **Watch item:** monitor WD's onboarding roadmap, SingPass requirements, and non-POCDEX agency expansion plans — these are the most likely triggers that would force a revisit of the identity architecture.
