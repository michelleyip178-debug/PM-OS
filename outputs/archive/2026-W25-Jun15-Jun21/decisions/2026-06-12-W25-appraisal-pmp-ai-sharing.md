## Competency mapping

### 1. People Development / Capability Building
- Delivered a hands-on session sharing my working AI stack: AI-powered ideation with Claude, rapid prototyping in Figma, and knowledge management in Notion.
- **75% of attendees reported greater clarity on AI** after the session. Baseline was low: most were only doing "ad-hoc prompts" (8 of 12) and 2 weren't using AI at all.
- Drove concrete, committed behavior change. Attendees' stated next steps included setting up Claude Code, building their own synthesis assistants, and automating user story generation and documentation.
- **Ripple effect beyond the room:** one attendee built a synthesis assistant after the session and shared it across their own team, multiplying the impact beyond direct attendees.

### 2. Innovation / Digital & AI Adoption
- Modeled what practical AI adoption looks like for a public-sector PM: a real, working stack rather than theory.
- Positioned AI as an everyday workflow tool, directly addressing the top pain points attendees named (stakeholder updates, data tracking across tools, PRDs).
- Set a reference point for the practice on what "good" AI-enabled PM work looks like, ahead of the curve relative to peers (0 attendees were at "automated/integrated" before the session).

### 3. Going Beyond Core Role
- This contribution was over-and-above my day responsibilities as Application Consultant, PSD.
- Volunteered time to prepare and present to the cross-agency PM community, contributing to the wider practice rather than just my own deliverables.
- Selected as a speaker, a signal of recognized expertise and trust to represent the practice.
