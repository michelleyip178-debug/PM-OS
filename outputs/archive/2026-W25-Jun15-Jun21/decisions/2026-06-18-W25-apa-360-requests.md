## How to use this

Paste each reviewer's prompt into AppraiseAI's 360 request flow. Each is tailored to what that person actually sees of my work, and the three questions map to the **L2 Schema dimensions** (Ownership · Strategic Alignment · Culture & Influence) so the responses slot straight into my [360 synthesis](2026-06-18-apa-360-feedback-synthesis.md).

**Standard structure for every reviewer** (keep it to 3 questions — short asks get answered):

1. A **strengths** question anchored to what they see most.
2. A **development** question (the honest one — where should I grow toward L3?).
3. A **specific-example** question (forces concrete evidence over vague praise).

## Shared intro (prepend to each, or set as the AppraiseAI request header)

> Hi [Name] — I'm gathering 360 input for my CY26 performance appraisal (first cycle as a PM). You see a specific side of how I work, so your view matters. Three short questions, ~5 minutes. Candid is more useful than kind — I'm especially after where I should grow toward the next level. Thank you.

---

## 1. Imelda — Fellow PM (peer)

*Lens: cross-cutting coordination, shared infra (WOG Auth, POCDEX), PM-to-PM craft.*

1. We coordinate across the Pathfinder features that touch shared ground (WOG Auth, POCDEX data flows, ringfencing). **Where do you see me owning my scope well**, and where does my work make yours easier or harder?
2. As a fellow PM, **where would you push me to level up** — scope discipline, stakeholder handling, the quality of my recommendations, prioritisation?
3. **One specific moment** where I either handled a cross-feature dependency well, or where it could have gone better — what happened?

---

## 2. Xian Zhang — Business Stakeholder / Decision-maker

*Lens: do I bring decision-ready recommendations? Do I represent the business need well?*

1. When I bring product or scope questions to you, **do I come with a clear recommendation and the tradeoffs**, or just options? How decision-ready is what I bring?
2. **Where could I serve the business need better** — understanding the problem, framing the call, or holding the right scope line for officers?
3. **One specific decision or design review** where my input genuinely helped (or where I left you doing the thinking I should have done) — which, and why?

---

## 3. Pow Hwee — Tech Lead

*Lens: scope clarity, ticket/AC quality, looping eng in early, technical-implication judgement.*

1. From a tech-lead seat, **how clear and well-scoped is the work I hand the team** — stories, ACs, grooming? Do I loop you in early enough on decisions with technical implications?
2. **Where do I create friction or rework** for engineering — ambiguous ACs, late scope changes, dependencies I should catch sooner?
3. **One specific instance** where my scoping or a decision clearly helped delivery (or caused avoidable churn) — what was it?

---

## 4. Daniel Wong — Peer / Cross-team

*Lens: how I show up across team boundaries — collaboration, reliability, clarity.*

1. Working across team lines with me, **what do I do well** — clarity, follow-through, making the collaboration work?
2. **Where should I grow** to be a stronger cross-team partner — communication, ownership of shared work, responsiveness?
3. **One specific interaction** that stuck with you, good or bad — what happened and what would have made it better?

---

## 5. Christopher — Peer / Cross-team

*Lens: cross-team collaboration, reliability, the quality of what I bring to shared work.*

1. In our cross-team work, **where do I add the most value**, and is what I bring (decisions, docs, asks) clear and reliable?
2. **Where would you challenge me to improve** as a cross-team collaborator or as a PM more broadly?
3. **One concrete example** — a time I helped unblock something, or a time the handoff/collaboration could have been cleaner?
