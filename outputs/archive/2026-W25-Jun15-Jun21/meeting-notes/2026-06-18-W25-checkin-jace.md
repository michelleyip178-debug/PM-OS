## Key Insights

**On KR framing:**
Jace's core push: every outcome needs a metric that can be evidenced at assessment time. "We did the work" is not enough — you need a number, a log, or a doc that proves the outcome landed.

**On auth/authorisation:**
Michelle's scope is the POCDEX side — whitelisting, auto-provisioning, integration — not WOG AD authentication itself. This distinction matters for framing what Michelle "owns" vs what other teams delivered.

**On process standardisation:**
Retro cadence is good raw material but needs to be reframed: name the specific SOP or process change, show what changed, and evidence the improvement. "We did retros" is not a KR; "we standardised the grooming ceremony and reduced planning prep time by X" is.

**On course aggregator:**
Jace challenged the problem statement directly. The fragmentation problem isn't obvious to end users if Cumulus already surfaces what they need. Michelle needs to articulate the specific gap before this can be a viable product direction.
