## What Changed

### 1. Login rate target — stays 20%, now confirmed as Q2–Q3'27

OTG's actual login rate for onboarded officers (last 180 days) is **14%**. The target was set at **20%** and that stays, but it's now explicitly pegged to Q2–Q3'27 (vs. whatever earlier milestone it may have implied).

**Implication:** The 20% login rate is aspirational above OTG's 14% baseline. It's achievable but not a given.

---

### 2. Competency profiles (40% with updated profiles) — pushed to Q3'27 only

The math chain that drove this change:
- 20% login rate over 12 months → **~40% of onboarded officers ever log in**
- If 40% update their competency profiles, that means **virtually every officer who logs in must update**
- Adrian flagged this as "pretty challenging" → now set as **Q3'27 target only**, not an earlier milestone

**Cross-reference with OKR file:** `otep-roadmap-okrs-2627.md` currently shows 40% at MVP (Oct'26) and 60% at Q3'27. This revision likely moves the 40% target out of MVP and into Q3'27 as a replacement. ⚠️ **OKR file will need updating once Mark/GK confirm.**

---

### 3. North Star (completed at least one development action) — lowered to 30%

CDG team is supportive of the "completed at least one development action" North Star framing. The target was lowered to **30%** using this logic:
- 40% of onboarded officers log in over 12 months
- Of those, **75% must complete a development action** to hit 30% of total onboarded officers
- Adrian's note: "this is a pretty steep KR"

**Cross-reference with OKR file:** R4 (Dec 2027) target is already 30% in the current file. This revision aligns — but confirms it applies at an earlier milestone. ✓ Consistent.
