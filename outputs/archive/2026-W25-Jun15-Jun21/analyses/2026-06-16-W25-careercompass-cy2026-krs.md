## Context

These KRs sit across two layers:

- **Product outcome KRs** — what officers and agencies experience. Derived from the IAA-approved OKR roadmap (MVP column, Dec 2026 milestone).
- **Delivery KRs** — how well the team is operating to hit those outcomes. Derived from OTEP Pathfinder delivery evidence (Apr–Jun 2026).

Both sets are needed: outcome KRs tell us if the product is working; delivery KRs tell us if the team is working well enough to get there.
