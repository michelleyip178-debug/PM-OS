## Decisions Made

1. **Compass will not wait for a perfect enterprise SSOT — mappings and reference tables are acceptable for MVP**
   - **Why:** HRPS, Cumulus, Compass Competency Bank, and POCDEX officer profile data are not perfectly aligned today, and HR systems can create job functions independently. Waiting for enterprise governance to resolve first would block MVP indefinitely.
   - **Impact:** Directly supersedes the earlier framing of this as an "SSOT exercise" (Pow Hwee explicitly cautioned against that framing). Reframes open item #18 as a mapping/reference-table problem, not a single-source-of-truth problem.

2. **Current Job Family dataset can be used as the working reference source while gaps are documented**
   - **Why:** Pragmatic unblock — waiting for a fully governed dataset isn't realistic on this timeline.
   - **Impact:** Rama to confirm with Chris whether the Excel file is the reference dataset (see Action Items) — this decision is provisional on that confirmation.

3. **Development continues despite agency-code short-form gaps; proper mapping introduced later**
   - **Why:** Standard short codes for agency data don't exist in production, despite multiple stakeholders assuming they did. Blocking development on this would stall the team.
   - **Impact:** Pow Hwee to work with Data Office on the mapping — this is a "fix it live" decision, not a resolved one.

4. **No SteerCo demo planned next week — immediate environment-preparation pressure removed**
   - **Why:** Team realized the upcoming SteerCo doesn't require a demo.
   - **Impact:** Frees GovTech/vendor teams to focus on QA readiness and data migration quality instead of presentation polish. Likely prevents avoidable shortcuts.

5. **Focus shifts to QA readiness and data cleanliness over demo readiness**
   - **Why:** Direct consequence of Decision 4 — no reason to rush environment work for a demo that isn't happening.
   - **Impact:** Rama + Pow Hwee's teams reprioritize accordingly.

6. **CMM and future releases should assume separate VAPT considerations where changes are "major"**
   - **Why:** Every major release likely requires its own VAPT cycle; CMM likely qualifies as major, and subsequent Opportunities releases may too.
   - **Impact:** No release-sequencing or overlapping-VAPT-window plan exists yet (see Risk 3) — this decision acknowledges the requirement without solving the scheduling problem it creates.

7. **Bidirectional competency synchronisation remains required scope, but should be presented separately from the CMM narrative**
   - **Why:** Adrian and Rama's recurring "what is CMM vs. what is Compass sync" debate — the team decided to at least separate the *narrative*, even though the scope boundary itself is still blurry.
   - **Impact:** Helps SteerCo positioning short-term, but doesn't resolve the underlying scope-separation problem flagged in What Didn't Go Well #3.

## Risks

### Not Fully Addressed

1. **Mapping debt will accumulate — Risk Rating: High**
   Every mismatch between POCDEX, HRPS, Cumulus, and the Competency Bank creates future maintenance overhead. The meeting acknowledged this but assigned no ownership for ongoing mapping governance — only for the initial Job Family/Function gap documentation (Rama). This is the same unresolved governance question as open item #18, now explicitly reframed as a "mapping debt" problem rather than an SSOT problem — the reframe doesn't reduce the risk, it just changes its name.

2. **Future competency synchronisation may be larger than expected — Risk Rating: High**
   Bidirectional sync back to HR systems is confirmed required scope, but discovery hasn't been done and HRPS APIs may need to be built from scratch (Cumulus uses EIB/batch mechanisms instead). This effort is not yet sized with any confidence — directly relevant to the CMM scope-pressure conversation already tracked in open item #50.

3. **VAPT may become a schedule bottleneck — Risk Rating: Medium-High**
   Every major release likely requires VAPT; CMM likely qualifies, and subsequent Opportunities releases may too. No release-sequencing, overlapping-VAPT-window, or branching-strategy discussion has happened yet. **Cross-references open item #39** (OTEP UAT/VAPT timeline: VAPT already scheduled 7 Sep–16 Oct for the current release) — if CMM needs its own VAPT window on top of this, the team needs to plan sequencing now, not after CMM scope is locked.

4. **Multiple teams sharing environments — Risk Rating: Medium**
   Shared data, shared environments, and possible override/conflict issues across multiple dev teams. Current mitigation is "keep channels open" — that's coordination, not governance, and doesn't scale as more teams onboard.

5. **Legal and AI governance workstream has just started — Risk Rating: Medium**
   GovTech Legal wants to discuss AI clauses for Compass Terms of Use. No timeline or dependency mapping exists yet — this could become a late-stage blocker if it surfaces close to go-live rather than now.
