## Key Discussion Points

### North Star: Application vs Completion

The central debate. No decision made — both options presented to SteerCo.

**Application metric** (within platform control)
- Counts officers clicking Apply
- Risk: overstates impact (50 applicants for 1 role = 50 "successes")

**Completion metric** (more meaningful outcome)
- Tracks actual placements
- Risk: depends on HR, agency practices, ATS — outside product team's control
- Implication: would require whole-of-PSD ownership, implicate HR policies, become a PSD-wide KPI

**Michelle's note:** The current OKR baseline is application-based (≥50% of STIP/Gig applications via OTEP by Month 3). If SteerCo shifts to completion, the entire measurement model changes and product accountability needs to be scoped accordingly.

### OKR Targets

Existing targets were flagged as overambitious given:
- Ongoing OTG → Compass transition workload
- Continuous agency onboarding resets maturity levels
- Limited team bandwidth

Key principle agreed: avoid targets that create false failure signals or overstretch teams. Example adjustment: 30% → ~20%.

Two-track approach: conservative for funding paper, aspirational for internal use.

### Transition Strategy (Critical Gap)

Mark's strongest input. The OTG → CareerCompass transition is not just a technical cutover. It requires change management, agency readiness, policy alignment, and clear trigger conditions for when Compass is "good enough."

Current state: no transition plan exists. Mark flagged this as the most important thing to produce before July SteerCo — ahead of feature delivery decisions.

Challenges:
- Dual system operations create duplicated effort (postings, learning festival, data tracking)
- OTG lacks application status tracking — Compass aims to provide it
- Agencies will transition at different speeds

### Gap Analysis (Escalated)

Mark raised this directly. Gap analysis was deprioritised without a clear rationale or timeline commitment, and he sees it as the core differentiator vs OTG. Without it, the platform lacks strategic value in the eyes of key stakeholders.

Current status: deprioritised with no owner, no timeline. Escalated for re-evaluation. ITC team to present options before July SteerCo.

Context from OKRs: the Q4 2027 target requires 40% of onboarded officers to have viewed competency gap analysis — this is a long-horizon OKR, but no roadmap slot exists for it yet.

### Dashboarding and Analytics

Tension between product team wanting to offload dashboard building and WD/PSD stakeholders wanting self-service agency insights. Resolved short-term as manual reports on request; long-term move toward centralised/democratised access.
