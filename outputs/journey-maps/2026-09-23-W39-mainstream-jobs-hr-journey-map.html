Where the real process lives: Careers@Gov, entirely

Posting authorship, applicant review, interviews, and the hire decision all happen on Careers@Gov, using whatever process and governance that platform already runs for a full, public-facing vacancy open beyond the public service. CareerCompass HR/Admin has no visibility into any of it, no audit log, no applicant record, no status to check on. There's nothing to design guardrails around because there's no CareerCompass-side process to guard.

What HR isn't doing here

No posting creation, no approval, no applicant screening, no decision workflow, no audit trail, no orphan-posting fallback. Every governance mechanism CareerCompass HR owns for STIPs & Gigs simply doesn't exist for this type, because CareerCompass never touches the process.

The one thing worth watching

Because CareerCompass has zero visibility past the deep-link, it also has no way to know if Careers@Gov changes its posting format, breaks the link, or retires the listing. There's no monitoring built for that today, it would need to be a deliberate addition, not an oversight to flag against current scope.

Why this map is one row, one phase: Mainstream Jobs is a redirect, confirmed in the R1 opportunity-type scope decision as "Listing label only" for discovery and "No — deep-link" for apply. There is no HR admin surface in CareerCompass to map for this type, that's the correct end state, not an incomplete journey.

Contrast with the other three types: IJR and SJR make HR the engine (screening, matching, approving). STIPs & Gigs makes HR the guardrail (RBAC, audit log, orphan fallback). Mainstream Jobs makes HR a bystander, the process is entirely Careers@Gov's, start to finish.
