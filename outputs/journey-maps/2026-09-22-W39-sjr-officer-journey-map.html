Officer journey · Structured Job Rotation (SJR / secondment)

How officers get seconded

A cross-agency move that runs once a year on PSD's calendar. Nomination comes before visibility, so officers can't browse roles until they're already in the cycle. Source: Rotation Guidebook (Feb 2026), Section 4 & Table 4.

R1 scope boundary: SJR-the-programme stays on OTG through the 2027 cycle and doesn't move to Compass in R1 — this journey isn't part of what R1 delivers. It migrates to Compass ahead of the 2028 cycle (risk register R-13, resolved). What does move to Compass in R1 is the broader Secondment umbrella's agency-led and officer-initiated paths, which is a different, separate journey.

During the secondment

The parent agency checks in regularly for the first 6 months. The borrowing agency handles onboarding and well-being check-ins. Once the officer has been there 6 months or more, the borrowing agency assesses their performance. Pay, benefits, and employment status stay with the parent agency the whole time.

Coming back, starting 6 months before the end

  • Parent agency HR schedules check-ins and reviews what the officer's picked up
  • Confirms what the officer wants next and shares which roles are open
  • Officer can ask to stay with the borrowing agency (decided case by case) or extend
  • If no role turns up despite a real effort, No Pay Leave is the fallback, by mutual agreement

The big difference from IJR: discovery here is nomination-gated, not open browsing. An officer can't see SJR roles on OTG until they've cleared eligibility and their agency HR has nominated them into that year's cycle (Annex B, Q7).

Promotion concession, from AY26/PY27: officers who rotate out and score 'C+' or better in their first year with the borrowing agency may be considered for promotion, at the agency's discretion. This doesn't apply to Internal Job Rotations.

One rule not shown above, and not something Compass checks: officers are only meant to be in one development programme at a time (Annex A, Q12), so this journey assumes they're not also in an IJR, Gig, or STIP track. This is Guidebook policy, not an R1 platform feature — Compass doesn't enforce or flag concurrent enrollment (confirmed 23 Sep, R-28).
