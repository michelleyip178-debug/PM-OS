What it takes to run a secondment cycle

Two HR functions work side by side on PSD's fixed calendar. Agency HR handles their own officers and roles; PSD and Functional Leaders broker the cross-agency match. Source: Rotation Guidebook (Feb 2026), Section 4.2–4.3, Table 4 & Annex F.

R1 scope boundary: SJR-the-programme stays on OTG through the 2027 cycle — this HR workflow doesn't move to Compass in R1, it migrates ahead of the 2028 cycle (risk register R-13, resolved). Compass's R1 scope covers the broader Secondment umbrella's agency-led and officer-initiated paths, a separate journey from PSD's annual SJR exercise shown here.

A liability that sits quietly with borrowing agency HR

If an officer is injured or dies while performing borrowing agency duties, the borrowing agency covers compensation (IM2F 6.3.7C), no less favourable than what the lending agency would have provided. There's no requirement to buy insurance for it, but the borrowing agency is expected to carry the liability one way or another, insured or not.

Where the load actually sits

Unlike IJR, where one agency does the matching alone, SJR splits the effort across three parties: parent HR, borrowing HR, and PSD/FLs, and none of them owns the whole pipeline. Re-integration planning alone takes 6 months and falls entirely on parent agency HR, long after PSD has stepped back.

Where it gets heaviest: nomination (Phase 02) is also role-sourcing. Agencies aren't just picking people, they're expected to find and submit roles too, ideally 2 per nominee. Re-integration (Phase 06) is the longest tail of all: it starts 6 months before the end and belongs entirely to the parent agency.

What PSD doesn't own: PSD and the FLs facilitate matching but can't guarantee it. If the SJR pool doesn't produce a fit, agencies are told to sort it out themselves (Annex B, Q11).

The constraint that's Guidebook policy, not an R1 platform feature: officers are only meant to be in one development programme at a time (Annex A, Q12), same as IJR, but harder for Agency HR to track manually here since it spans two workflows (agency-internal and WOG-central) instead of one. Compass doesn't check or enforce this in R1 (confirmed 23 Sep, R-28).
