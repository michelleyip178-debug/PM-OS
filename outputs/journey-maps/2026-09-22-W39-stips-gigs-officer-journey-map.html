Finding and applying for a gig

Self-serve on both sides: any officer can post, any officer can apply, HR has zero role anywhere in this flow (confirmed 23 Sep, R-27). It's the fastest of the mobility paths by design, under 2 minutes to apply. Source: STIPs & Gigs scope map, 22 Sep 2026.

If they're offered the role

The badge switches to Offered right away. Everything after that is manual: poster and applicant sort out onboarding off-platform. No contract generation, no formal HR placement workflow.

If they're not

The badge switches to Not Selected. There's no automated regret email or feedback loop in scope, so the badge is the only signal the applicant gets.

What's different from IJR and SJR: no eligibility screening before discovery, no HR-run matching exercise, no ranked preferences. One application, reviewed by one poster (plus up to 2 co-evaluators), decided with a single click.

Four things not locked in: the Reporting Officer awareness checkbox, 30-day auto-expiry, the under-2-minute apply target, and the instant poster alert email. The mechanics are set, it's these specifics that are still pending.

Post-offer is out of scope and treated as an explicit, manual, off-platform step, not a gap to close later by default.
