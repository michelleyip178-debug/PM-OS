Why IJR and SJR are neighbours, not the same box. Both are nomination-gated, HR-run, temporary moves of about two years, the opposite of STIPs & Gigs' self-serve model, which is why they sit in the same "Role change" branch. The confirmed split between them is scope: IJR stays within the same agency, SJR crosses agencies. They no longer share a migration path: IJR is confirmed R1 scope, building natively in Compass end to end (including in-app HR review), decoupled from SJR's OTG-module reasoning. SJR alone stays on OTG through 2027 and migrates ahead of 2028, on its own independent timing. Full detail: risk register R-25 (PM-OS workspace), fully resolved.

"SJR" and "secondment" aren't quite the same thing. Secondment is the umbrella: any temporary cross-agency move. SJR is specifically PSD's centrally-run, annual version of it. Agency-led and officer-initiated secondments are real, separate paths, not variations on SJR.

Internal Jobs is the odd one out here — it's shown as a target-state architecture, not a current process. AS-IS, Job IDs and competency data are split across Cumulus and HRPS, synced via a one-way push from Cumulus into HRPS. TO-BE, Compass pulls from HRPS only, at regular sync intervals — a periodic read, not a live two-way API, and Compass doesn't hold or maintain the data itself (corrected 23 Sep, after a misread of the source diagram; Compass never talks to Cumulus directly). Apply is a confirmed redirect to whichever system hosts the posting, not TBC. The open question now is whether HRPS supplies ringfencing data or Compass has to build it (R-07).

The pattern: IJR and SJR both run through HR, with eligibility checks and matching that take weeks, whichever path you take. STIPs & Gigs is self-serve on both ends, and HR's job there is zero, not smaller (confirmed 23 Sep, R-27). RBAC, an audit log, and a fallback if a posting gets orphaned still need to happen, they just don't have an owner right now, that's an open gap, not a resolved HR function. Internal Jobs sits apart from both patterns — it's not self-serve and it's not a development programme, it's a data-architecture problem dressed as a posting type.

True for IJR, SJR, and STIPs & Gigs, as Rotation Guidebook policy — not as something R1 implements: one programme at a time (Annex A, Q12). Compass doesn't check or enforce this in R1 (confirmed 23 Sep, R-28) — it's a policy constraint officers and HR are expected to follow, not a platform guardrail. Internal Jobs isn't part of that rule either way — it's a permanent move, not a development-programme slot.

IJR and Internal Jobs are closer in shape than the rows above might suggest, per BO confirmation (23 Sep): duration, entry paths, process time, and HR ownership are the same across both. The one real difference is ringfencing type — Internal Jobs is a flat agency-level check (any officer in the agency can see and apply), IJR can layer on agency-defined per-officer eligibility criteria (e.g. minimum tenure in a role).
