What happens on Careers@Gov, invisible to CareerCompass

Viewing the full posting, applying, HR review, interviews, and the hire/no-hire decision all happen on Careers@Gov. It's a full public-facing posting, open beyond the public service, so it was never CareerCompass's process to run. CareerCompass has no apply flow, no status badge, and no record of the outcome for this type.

Why this journey is this short: Mainstream Jobs are authored and decided entirely in Careers@Gov. CareerCompass's role is a listing label plus a deep-link, nothing more. There's no native apply, no status tracking, and no HR role in CareerCompass for this type at all.

Contrast with STIPs & Gigs, IJR, and SJR: those types all have some native footprint in CareerCompass, discovery at minimum, apply and track for STIPs/Gigs. Mainstream Jobs has none of that. It's a pure redirect, the "Listing label only" / "No — deep-link" delivery mode confirmed in the R1 opportunity-type scope decision.
