# Opportunities Document Map — All Six Types

Twelve journey maps (an officer view and an HR view for each of six opportunity types), plus two comparison views, all built to the same visual system so they read together. Each journey map traces one type end to end from one persona's seat. The two comparisons put multiple types side by side, one scoped to the mobility-programme paths (IJR/SJR/STIPs & Gigs/Internal Jobs), the other covering the full Opportunities module with no release boundary.

**The six types:** Internal Job Rotation (IJR), Structured Job Rotation / Secondment (SJR), STIPs & Gigs, Internal Jobs, Mainstream Jobs (Careers@Gov). IJR and SJR each split into two real paths within their own journey maps (Structured vs. Officer-Initiated; PSD Annual vs. Agency-Led/Initiated). Internal Jobs splits into AS-IS vs. TO-BE, since it's a live architecture transition, not two concurrent paths.

## Why the shapes differ

Each map's structure is itself a finding, not just a design choice:

- **IJR (officer)** splits into two lanes (Structured vs. Officer-Initiated) because both are real, parallel paths within one agency. Officer-Initiated still runs through supervisor endorsement and HR screening, so "any time" never means self-serve.
- **IJR (HR)** splits into Agency HR vs. Releasing/Receiving Division — HR owns the full pipeline (eligibility, pooling, matching, unmatched-officer follow-up); divisions engage only at specific moments.
- **SJR (officer and HR)** both add a calendar strip (Mar → Sep) because the programme runs on PSD's fixed annual whole-of-government cycle. Officer-side, the defining mechanic is nomination-gated discovery. HR-side, effort splits three ways — parent agency HR, borrowing agency HR, and PSD/Functional Leaders — with no single pipeline owner, and re-integration alone runs 6 months, entirely on parent-agency HR.
- **STIPs & Gigs (officer)** is the only one with mirrored applicant/poster lanes instead of path variants, since both sides are genuinely self-serve and symmetrical.
- **STIPs & Gigs (HR)** is structurally the opposite of IJR/SJR HR: HR was confirmed to have zero role here on 23 Sep, against a native-apply model. **Reverted again 25 Sep (R-27, third and final pass):** apply is confirmed unchanged from MVP, uniform FormSG-extraction for every agency — the pilot/non-pilot split floated earlier the same day is withdrawn. Zero-HR-role now holds cleanly with no caveat — there's no in-app applicant data anywhere in this model, for any agency, so there's no RBAC or applicant-data question to design for at all.
- **Internal Jobs (officer and HR)** use an AS-IS/TO-BE lane pairing instead of two concurrent paths — this is a sequential architecture transition (Cumulus/HRPS split today, synced via POCDEX → Compass as the maintained data hub, POCDEX retired), not two live options an officer or HR chooses between. Even in the TO-BE lane, whether officers can actually post or apply for Internal Jobs inside Compass is marked TBC — the data architecture is ahead of the product decision.
- **Mainstream Jobs (officer and HR)** are deliberately the shortest maps in the set. It's a pure redirect to Careers@Gov: CareerCompass shows a listing label and a deep-link, and everything else (apply, review, decision) happens entirely outside CareerCompass. The HR journey has almost no content because CareerCompass HR/Admin has no role in this type at all.

## R1 scope, per type

These maps show full end-state journeys; R1 delivers a subset. Per the risk register (`2026-09-16-W38-r1-risk-register.md`) as of 25 Sep, **reflecting the 25 Sep architecture reversal (R-07, R-25, R-31) and the third-pass STIPs & Gigs correction (R-27, FormSG-uniform confirmed final):**

| Type | What R1 actually delivers | What's NOT R1 |
|---|---|---|
| STIPs & Gigs | **Reverted again 25 Sep, third and final pass — pilot/non-pilot split withdrawn, uniform FormSG confirmed.** Discovery is native to Compass, WOG-wide, for everyone (R-14, unaffected). Apply is unchanged from MVP, for every agency: Compass extracts the FormSG link from the OTG posting's description and shows it as the Apply button; if no link exists, the button is disabled and the officer is told to contact the poster directly | Native creation/posting in Compass, for any agency — stays OTG-only, no exceptions. Native in-app apply for any agency — none exists anywhere in this model. Zero-HR-role (R-27) holds cleanly, no caveat — no in-app applicant data exists for any agency |
| Secondment (agency-led / officer-initiated paths) | Discovery on Compass, redirect to OTG or the hosting HR system to apply (R-13). Unaffected in direction by the reversal — this was already redirect-based | Cross-HR-system auth isn't solved (R-24, 🔴) — an officer can see a listing they can't act on |
| SJR (PSD's annual programme specifically) | Nothing — stays on OTG through the 2027 cycle, unaffected by the reversal | The entire SJR journey shown in this set; migrates to Compass ahead of 2028 |
| Internal Jobs | Discovery only, surfaced in Compass's unified catalog, sourced from OTG (fed upstream by HRPS/Cumulus). **R-07 down to 🟠 Amber, 25 Sep** — HRPS API blocker moot, Compass no longer builds direct HRPS integration | Native apply — apply is a confirmed redirect to OTG. Open question: whether Compass can deep-link to a specific posting or only the general OTG landing page |
| Internal Job Rotation (IJR) | **Reversed 25 Sep (R-25) — grouped with Internal Jobs, not STIPs & Gigs.** Discovery only, ringfenced per agency plus optional per-officer eligibility criteria. Redirect to OTG to apply | Self-serve creation, native apply, in-app HR applicant review, officer status tracking, in-app decision/matching — all confirmed 24 Sep, all reversed 25 Sep. See [Epic D One-Pager](../prds/2026-09-24-W39-epic-d-ijr-scoping-one-pager.md), rewritten a fourth time |
| Mainstream Jobs | Redirect only — listing label + deep-link to Careers@Gov. Unaffected by the reversal | This is already the type's full scope, R1 or otherwise; no expansion planned |

**Pattern, as of the 25 Sep corrections (third and final pass on STIPs & Gigs):** Compass takes over *discovery* across every R1 type, WOG-wide — that much is uniform. *Authoring* stays wherever it lives today, without exception: OTG for STIPs & Gigs postings, OTG/HRPS/Cumulus for Internal Jobs and IJR, the hosting HR system for Secondment, OTG through 2028 for SJR. *Apply* is now uniform too, in the sense that every type routes off-platform: Internal Jobs, IJR, and Secondment redirect to a general landing page or source system; STIPs & Gigs deep-links directly to the posting's extracted FormSG form, or shows a disabled button if none exists. No type has native in-app apply, for any agency. The "officer leaves Compass, we lose visibility" problem this release was built to fix is not solved for any opportunity type in R1 — Compass narrows the gap for STIPs & Gigs by getting officers to the right external form faster, but the apply and outcome loop still closes entirely outside Compass.
