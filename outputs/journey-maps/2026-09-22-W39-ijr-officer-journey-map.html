Officer journey · Internal Job Rotation (IJR)

How officers get into an IJR

Two ways in — a scheduled agency cycle, or an ad-hoc request that runs on approvals instead of a calendar. Source: Rotation Guidebook (Feb 2026), Section 3 & Annex C.

R1 scope boundary: confirmed R1 scope, resolved 24 Sep (risk register R-25). IJR gets the same delivery shape as STIPs & Gigs, end to end: self-serve creation, native discovery, apply via a structured form, in-app applicant review for Agency HR, and officer status tracking, all inside Compass. The one thing that stays offline is the matching/assignment decision itself, if IJR still needs to weigh multiple applicants against multiple roles at once rather than a simple per-applicant offer or reject. This decouples IJR from SJR: SJR alone stays on OTG through 2027, migrating ahead of 2028 on its own timing. What's below is the current OTG-era process, kept for historical reference. The apply step (phase 04, "Making a case") and the matching step (phase 05) don't carry over as drawn: the confirmed Compass flow is a structured form for apply, and an in-app review table for HR, not "discuss with cluster/division, submit ranked preferences" or an entirely offline process, so both need a redesign pass. Record of the decision: risk register R-25 (PM-OS workspace), fully resolved, ready for Epic D story-drafting.

If it's a match

The outcome gets confirmed in about 4 weeks. Handover and admin setup follow over 4 to 16 weeks. Agencies try to line up start dates to a common window, and the officer begins the new role.

If it's not (structured path)

  • Join the IJR waiting list
  • Try again next year's cycle
  • Go for SJR the following year instead
  • Look for roles in other agencies

After two years without a match, HR is expected to step up support.

How long it takes, structured path: about 28 weeks, roughly 7 months, from nomination to the start of transition. That's from the phase durations in the guidebook's Table 2 and Annex C.

The officer-initiated path has no set timeline, but every step still goes through a person: supervisor endorsement, HR screening, a division interview. Neither path is "apply and go."

One rule not shown above, and not something Compass checks: officers are only meant to be in one development programme at a time (Annex A, Q12), so this journey assumes they're not also in a Gig, STIP, or SJR track. This is Guidebook policy, not an R1 platform feature — Compass doesn't enforce or flag concurrent enrollment (confirmed 23 Sep, R-28).
