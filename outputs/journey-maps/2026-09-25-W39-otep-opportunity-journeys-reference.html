1 · Current state

Today

STIPs & Gigs discovery and FormSG-extraction apply are already live in Compass, shipped in MVP. Internal jobs, IJR and Secondment are split across OTG, HRPS, Cumulus and each listing's own hosting HR system, each with its own rules and application process.

2 · R1 Pathfinder

Agreed approachTarget Feb/Mar

Compass extends the discovery layer it already has for STIPs & Gigs to Internal Jobs, IJR and Secondment. Every Apply sends the officer back to the source system. Internal Jobs is currently blocked, no committed HRPS API delivery date; IJR and Secondment are groomable now.
