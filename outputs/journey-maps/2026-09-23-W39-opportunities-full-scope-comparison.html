Cross-cutting
Officers can only be in one development programme at a time — applies to STIPs, Gigs, IJR, and SJR alike (Rotation Guidebook, Annex A, Q12). Internal Jobs and Mainstream Jobs are full moves, not development-programme slots, so this rule doesn't constrain them the same way.

Internal Jobs is shown here as a target-state architecture, not today's process — worth reading differently from the other five rows. Corrected 23 Sep after a misread of the source diagram. AS-IS, Job IDs and competency data are split across Cumulus and HRPS, synced through POCDEX, with CareerCompass only a downstream consumer. In the target state, Cumulus pushes postings into HRPS's Internal Job Portal one-way (Compass has no direct relationship with Cumulus at all), and Compass pulls from HRPS only, at regular sync intervals, not a live two-way API. Compass never holds or maintains the data itself, maintenance stays entirely in Cumulus/HRPS. POCDEX is still retired in the target state, that part is unaffected. Apply is a confirmed redirect: the officer links out to whichever system, HRPS or Cumulus, actually hosts the posting. The open question is no longer whether officers can apply in Compass, it's whether HRPS supplies ringfencing/agency-tagging data directly or Compass has to build that logic itself.

Why STIPs and Gigs still look different from the rest: they're the only types where the actual adoption blocker (agencies can't customise forms on existing systems, so they double-post across FormSG, Careers@Gov, and OTG) is something native creation in CareerCompass directly solves. Mainstream Jobs, IJR, and SJR don't have that same problem — their friction is elsewhere (eligibility data, coordinated matching, cross-agency governance), so pulling their authoring into CareerCompass wouldn't fix anything. Internal Jobs is the exception: its target-state move to CareerCompass is a data-architecture decision, not an adoption fix.

None of Mainstream Jobs, SJR, or Internal Jobs have CareerCompass write-back, by architecture, not by release choice. Mainstream Jobs and SJR remain a one-time port with no ongoing sync. Internal Jobs is a periodic read-only pull from HRPS, corrected 23 Sep from an earlier framing that implied Compass would push data back — it doesn't, and there's no plan for it to.

"Native creation" for STIPs/Gigs means template-based, not full free-form authoring. Full native creation for every agency and every type is a further-out ambition, not part of this end-state picture as scoped today.

IJR and Internal Jobs are more alike than the two rows above might suggest, per BO confirmation (23 Sep): duration, entry paths, browsing model, process time, and HR ownership are the same shape across both. The one real difference is ringfencing type — Internal Jobs is a flat agency-level check, IJR can add agency-defined per-officer eligibility criteria on top (tenure-in-role being one example, not necessarily the only one). Don't read the other rows as implying a bigger structural gap between the two than that.
