R1 Scope Decision · Opportunities

R1 scope, confirmed 23 Sep

Adrian's R1 scope slide ("STIPs & Gigs | Internal Jobs, SJRs & Secondments") answers the question that stalled Tuesday's estimation session: who can discover, create, and apply. Internal Job Rotation (IJR) sat outside that slide; confirmed R1 scope, resolved 24 Sep, same delivery shape as STIPs & Gigs end to end (see IJR card below). This is the full picture, laid out by type, with what's resolved and what isn't.

● Resolves R-14 / R-23 / R-25 Source: Adrian Ang's R1 scope slide (IJR not covered — confirmed separately, see R-25)
The headline decision: population is WOG-wide, not pilot-only. STIPs & Gigs open to all WOG officers via POCDEX integration. Compass becomes the sole platform for posting, discovering, and applying to STIPs, Gigs, Internal Jobs, and Secondment. SJR itself is the one exception — it stays on OTG through 2027. IJR is in this too: confirmed R1 scope, same delivery shape as STIPs & Gigs end to end, including in-app HR applicant review. Only the matching/assignment decision itself stays offline (R-25).

Two distinctions worth getting right

Both of these are places where "WOG-wide" quietly means something different depending on the type — easy to misread if the slide (or this decision) gets summarized too quickly. The second one is now a live R1 design question, not a 2028-migration one.

SJR — the programme

PSD's specific, centrally-run, annual secondment exercise (Mar–Sep, MX12-10 grade, nomination-gated). This is what stays on OTG through 2027.

≠
Secondment — the umbrella

Any temporary cross-agency move, including agency-led and officer-initiated paths outside PSD's annual exercise. This is what the slide moves to Compass now, for discovery and application on the consolidated page.

IJR — WOG-wide catalog

Now that IJR is native in Compass for R1, any officer anywhere in government would be able to see that IJR opportunities exist and browse the unified catalog. That's the "WOG-wide" part of IJR's R1 scope.

≠
IJR — agency-scoped eligibility

IJR is a within-agency rotation by definition — it has no cross-agency form. Eligibility and applicant review still resolve to the officer's own agency, now handled in-app by Agency HR; only the final matching/assignment decision stays offline. WOG-wide visibility of the catalog is not the same as WOG-wide eligibility for the rotation itself. This is a live R1 design question for the structured apply form (R-25), not a 2028 one.

What this resolves

Three risks close out on the back of this slide and the same-day IJR confirmation.

R-14

Pilot-only vs. WOG-wide discovery

Resolved. This was the single highest-leverage open question in R1 — nearly every scope, architecture, GTM, and estimation question depended on it. Answer: WOG-wide, via POCDEX.

R-23

No agreed product narrative

Resolved. Adrian's decisions existed only as informally-referenced Slack threads before this. The slide is now the documented artifact — fold it into the one-pager and reduced-scope brief so it doesn't get re-litigated a third time.

R-25

IJR's delivery shape is confirmed

Resolved. IJR follows the STIPs & Gigs model end to end: self-serve creation, native discovery, structured-form apply, in-app applicant review for Agency HR (new), and officer status tracking. Decoupled from SJR — SJR alone stays on OTG through 2027, migrating ahead of 2028 on its own timing. Epic D can now be drafted with the same story shapes as Epic A. One nuance to confirm with Adrian: whether the Decision stage stays a simple per-applicant offer/reject or needs to preserve a pooled-matching shape (multiple applicants weighed against multiple roles at once) — that piece stays offline either way for now.

What this doesn't resolve

Two things need attention before this scope goes any further up the chain.

R-24 · new

No cross-HR-system authentication

The gap: if an officer clicks an opportunity hosted on an HR system they don't have access to, they can't proceed. Adrian's slide states this plainly as a known constraint, and punts the fix to "once Workable becomes the WOG ATS" — a 2027+ track, outside R1.

Why it matters more now: Compass is now positioned as the WOG-wide "consolidated Opportunities page." A consolidated catalog that silently dead-ends on some listings undercuts the exact narrative this decision just confirmed. No R1 mitigation is currently proposed.

R-07 / D-01 · escalated

HRPS API still undelivered

The gap: Internal Jobs scope depends on HRPS delivering API access for the periodic pull Compass needs. Architecture corrected 23 Sep: Compass pulls from HRPS only, not a live two-way API, and doesn't hold the data itself. The open question sharpened to one specific thing: does HRPS's API response supply ringfencing/agency-tagging data directly, or does Compass need to derive/build that logic itself. The slide names the underlying dependency as "critical" but attaches no delivery date.

Why it matters more now: this was previously an internal fallback trigger (defer to R2 if it doesn't land). Now that Internal Jobs is committed, communicated scope, an unresolved API is a gap in already-stated scope, not just a quiet contingency.

Where this leaves the estimate: the SJR delivery-mechanism decision (D-02) and RBAC sizing groundwork (D-06) are both closed as of 23 Sep — this decision was the thing blocking them. That doesn't mean the estimate itself is unblocked: it still needs to be re-run against this newly-confirmed scope (nothing currently circulating was sized against it, see R-12), CAM (R-15) is a separate, still-open three-way conflict, and R-24/R-07 above are blockers this decision introduces or reopens. IJR (R-25) is fully resolved and in the estimate, ready for Epic D story-drafting.
