## 1. Journey Scope & Personas

### Persona 1: The Public Officer (Applicant / Explorer)
* **Archetype:** "Lane 1: Intentional Mover" (mid-career officer looking for cross-agency growth) and "Lane 2: Passive Watcher" (curious officer exploring gigs).
* **Goal:** Discover public sector opportunities in one place and apply without bureaucratic friction or retyping profile data.
* **Time Horizon:** 15 minutes (discovery & apply) to 2 weeks (outcome resolution).

### Persona 2: The Posting Creator (Any Officer with a Task / Gig)
* **Archetype:** Project lead, team manager, or HR officer across the 6 pilot agencies needing extra hands for a sprint, project, or task.
* **Goal:** Post an internal gig in minutes, find verified talent, and select candidates without touching a spreadsheet or buried email thread.
* **Time Horizon:** 5 minutes (posting) to 14 days (review & offer).

## 3. Journey Map: The Posting Creator (Hiring Manager / Project Lead)

```
Emotional Curve:
Resource-strapped (😟) ──> Empowered (😊 4-field post) ──> Anticipatory (😐) ──> Delighted (🎉 Applicant Review Table) ──> Resolved (✨ 1-Click Offer)
```

| Phase | 1. Identify Need & Initiate | 2. Author & Publish Gig | 3. Receive Applications | 4. Review Candidates | 5. Decide & Onboard | 6. Close Vacancy & Retention |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase Goal** | Find capable help for an urgent sprint, project, or task. | Post a gig in under 5 minutes without HR bureaucracy. | Get alerted immediately when candidates apply. | Screen candidate skills and profiles without spreadsheets. | Make a clean Offer or Rejection decision. | Unlist the opportunity and comply with data privacy policies. |
| **Touchpoints** | • Team backlog / sprint planning<br>• CareerCompass Header CTA (`+ Post a Gig`) | • Creation Modal (`G-01`)<br>• Competency pill selector<br>• Mandatory RO checkbox | • GovTech Postman automated transactional email alert | • "My Posted Gigs" dashboard<br>• Applicant Review Table (`G-05`)<br>• Expandable candidate rows | • Action buttons (`Offer` / `Reject`)<br>• Confirmation Dialog (`G-06`)<br>• Direct email / Teams | • `Close Vacancy` button<br>• Automated 30-day expiration cron<br>• 90-day retention purge worker |
| **User Actions** | 1. Decides to get cross-agency support.<br>2. Logs into Compass.<br>3. Clicks `+ Post a Gig`. | 1. Fills role title & description.<br>2. Selects 3-4 target skills.<br>3. Adds co-evaluator emails.<br>4. Ticks RO awareness box.<br>5. Clicks `Publish`. | 1. Receives email notification: *"New candidate applied"*. | 1. Logs into Compass.<br>2. Opens "My Posted Gigs".<br>3. Reviews applicant table.<br>4. Expands rows to read answers. | 1. Compares top candidates.<br>2. Clicks `Offer` on selected officer.<br>3. Clicks `Reject` on remaining.<br>4. Sends welcome email on Teams. | 1. Clicks `Close Vacancy`.<br>2. Posting unlists from catalog.<br>3. System schedules 90-day data purge. |
| **User Thoughts** | *"I don't have headcount to hire full-time, but I need 5 hours a week of UX research."* | *"I don't want to spend 2 weeks waiting for central HR to approve my post."* | *"Great, someone applied already. Let me check their profile."* | *"No messy Excel trackers or detached PDF resumes in my inbox. Everything is right here."* | *"I'll offer the gig to Sarah and connect on Teams this afternoon to kick off."* | *"Role is filled. Glad I don't have to worry about GDPR or keeping resumes on my desktop."* |
| **Emotions** | 😟 Stressed about workload<br>Needing quick support | 😊 Empowered<br>Relieved by simple 4-field form | 😃 Pleased<br>Excited by early candidate traction | 😎 In control<br>Appreciating the clean decision table | 🎉 Decisive & Relieved<br>Work is staffed without red tape | 😌 Peace of mind<br>Clean governance compliance |
| **Pain Points** | 🔴 Strict headcount limits for full-time hires.<br>🔴 Informal channels (chat groups, word of mouth) yield low reach. | 🔴 Complex HR approval chains and administrative gatekeeping.<br>🔴 Designing custom questionnaires. | 🔴 Missing candidate submissions buried in general inboxes. | 🔴 Downloading multiple FormSG CSVs.<br>🔴 Manually cross-referencing candidate skills. | 🔴 Writing manual rejection emails to unselected candidates.<br>🔴 Uncertainty on how to handle offer approvals. | 🔴 Forgetting to take down filled postings (Ghost Gigs).<br>🔴 Data liability of retaining officer CVs indefinitely. |
| **Compass Solution** | 💡 **Micro-Gig Marketplace:** Unlocks fractional cross-agency project help. | 💡 **Self-Serve Open Posting:** Any authenticated officer can post; single RO declaration replaces approval queues. | 💡 **Instant Transactional Alerts:** Direct email to poster via Postman REST API. | 💡 **In-App Applicant Review Table:** Displays decision-critical columns (Name, Agency, Skills Match) with 1-click expand. | 💡 **1-Click Selection (Offer/Reject):** Updates in-app status badge automatically; moves onboarding logistics to Teams. | 💡 **Automated Lifecycle Governance:** 30-day auto-expiry prevents ghost gigs; automated 90-day hard data purge. |
| **Key Metrics** | • Pilot agency posting volume<br>• Active opportunities (target: 20-30) | • Time to publish (target: <5 mins)<br>• % posts with co-evaluators | • Time to first applicant (target: ≤5 days) | • Average applicants per gig (target: ≥3)<br>• Applicant review rate | • Time to offer decision (target: ≤14 days)<br>• Offer acceptance rate | • Vacancy fill rate<br>• Data retention compliance (100% purged at 90d) |
