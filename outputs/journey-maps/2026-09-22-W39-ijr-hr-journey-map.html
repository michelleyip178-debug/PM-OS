What it takes to run an IJR cycle

Agency HR carries the whole thing: eligibility, pooling, matching, and every officer left unmatched at the end. Receiving divisions carry a lighter load alongside them. Source: Rotation Guidebook (Feb 2026), Section 3.2 & Table 2.

R1 scope boundary: reversed 25 Sep, Mark & GK's confirmed direction. IJR is discovery-only in Compass, grouped with Internal Jobs: postings continue in OTG, Compass surfaces them for discovery, and officers redirect back to OTG to apply. None of the native-Compass work confirmed 24 Sep applies — no self-serve creation, no in-app applicant review, no in-app decision/matching interface. R1 doesn't touch HR's process at all: eligibility checks, pooling, and matching stay exactly as they run today, entirely on OTG-adjacent tooling. What HR is on the hook for, beyond one cycle

Agencies are expected to keep participation balanced across divisions, keep completion rates high, and make sure no single division dominates placements while others get left out. That's HR's job to watch, not just each cycle's individual outcome.

Where the load actually sits

Eligibility checks, pulling roles together, matching, approvals, following up on unmatched officers, reviewing metrics: all of it sits with Agency HR. Divisions show up at specific moments; HR carries it end to end, cycle after cycle, with nothing beyond posting to OTG to help.

Where it gets heaviest: matching (Phase 04) is the highest-load phase. It's a manual balancing act between what officers want and what the organisation needs, not something rules can assign automatically.

It doesn't end at placement: unmatched officers stay an open relationship with HR: waitlist, retry next cycle, or a redirect toward SJR. After two years without a match, HR is expected to step up support (Table 2 / Annex C, unsuccessful outcome branch).

One constraint that's Guidebook policy, not an R1 platform feature: officers are only meant to be in one development programme at a time (Annex A, Q12), so HR is expected to help people choose between IJR, SJR, Gigs, and STIPs. Compass doesn't check or enforce this in R1 (confirmed 23 Sep, R-28) — it's on HR to manage manually, same as today.
