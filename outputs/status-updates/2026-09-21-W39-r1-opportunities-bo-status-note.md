# R1 Opportunities: Where Scope Stands

**So what:** R1's core plan hasn't changed. Three design questions came up this week that could widen scope slightly in specific areas. None of them threaten the release, and we'll have answers before the team finalizes the delivery estimate on Tuesday.

---

## What's Confirmed

- **STIPs & Gigs** (officers posting and applying to gigs/short-term postings) stays the centerpiece of R1, largely as planned.
- **How we pull public job listings into CareerCompass** (Careers@Gov integration) is locked. No changes there.
- **Saved Jobs, account management integration, and usage tracking** are all proceeding as planned, no open questions.

## What's Being Finalized This Week

Three design questions surfaced this week. Each one is about *who gets access* or *how one job type gets built*, not about whether R1 ships or what it fundamentally delivers.

1. **Should CareerCompass access for gig posting/applying extend beyond the 6 pilot agencies?**
   We're exploring whether officers government-wide (not just the 6 pilot agencies) could get access to just the gig-posting and application feature, without changing anything else about how the pilot works. This would be additive, not a replacement for the pilot structure.

2. **How should Structured Job Rotation (SJR) postings work in CareerCompass?**
   SJR currently runs entirely on the outgoing OTG platform. We're deciding whether CareerCompass shows SJR postings for browsing only (with officers applying on the existing system), or builds a fuller in-app experience for SJR specifically. This is being scoped as its own piece of work, separate from the core R1 build, so it won't affect R1's main timeline either way.

3. **Minor access-control addition to support #1 above**, if that's confirmed.

## What This Means for Timeline and Effort

The team is finalizing the delivery estimate this week. These three questions will be resolved before that number is presented, so the estimate reflects real scope, not a placeholder. We're not aware of anything here that changes the overall shape or risk profile of R1 — these are refinements to a small part of the plan, not a redesign.
