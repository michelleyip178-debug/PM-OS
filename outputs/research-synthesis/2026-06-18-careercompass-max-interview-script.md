# Interview Script: CareerCompass + Max Integration Research
**Date:** 2026-06-18
**Prepared by:** Michelle (CareerCompass / OTEP, Pathfinder team)
**Session length:** 30–40 min
**Audience:** WOG public service officers (all career stages)

---

## Research objectives

Three OST assumptions to validate:

| # | Assumption | Branch |
|---|-----------|--------|
| A1 | Officers who reflect on their career (via Max or otherwise) have no clear next step and don't end up on CareerCompass | No bridge |
| A2 | Officers don't know what development action is right for them — direction is unclear before they can act | Clarity gap |
| A3 | Applying for STIPs/Gigs feels like too big a commitment — officers stall before they start | Commitment friction |

Decisions this research will inform:
- Whether a CTA from Max → CareerCompass is the right first integration
- What form that CTA/handoff should take (generic vs personalised, link vs summary)
- Whether CareerCompass needs a lower-commitment entry point (e.g. bookmark/save) before "apply"

---

## Script

### Opening (2 min)

> "Thanks for making time. I'm Michelle, a product manager working on CareerCompass — the platform we're building to help officers explore opportunities and development resources in one place.
>
> I'm here to learn, not to pitch anything. There are no right or wrong answers — honest reactions are way more useful than polite ones. We're trying to understand how officers actually think about their career development, not how they think they should.
>
> Mind if I take notes? [If recording: is it okay if I record this?]
>
> We have about 35 minutes — does that still work for you?"

---

### Warm-up: role and context (5 min)

- "Tell me about your role — what does a typical week look like for you?"
- "How long have you been in the public service?"
- "When you think about your career, how actively are you thinking about what's next — is it something on your mind, or more in the background?"

*Goal: understand career stage, level of active engagement, and vocabulary they use.*

---

### Core: career development behaviour (10 min)

Start with a specific past event, not opinions.

**Anchor on the last real moment:**
- "Think back to the last time you actively did something about your career development — could be anything: reading about a role, talking to someone, signing up for a course. When was that?"
- "Walk me through what happened. What triggered it?"
- "What did you actually do? How did it go?"

**Probe the moment it stalled (if it did):**
- "Was there a point where you thought about doing something but then didn't? What happened there?"
- "What was the hardest part about taking that step?"
- "What did you try? What got in the way?"

**Understand what "good" looks like to them:**
- "If your career development was going really well, what would that look like day-to-day?"
- "How would you know?"

---

### Core: Max usage (if applicable) (8 min)

*Only go here if they've used Max. If not, ask about any chatbot or career tool they've tried.*

- "Have you come across Max — the career coaching chatbot on AIBots? [If yes:] Tell me about the last time you used it."
- "What were you trying to figure out when you opened it?"
- "Walk me through what you did. What happened after the conversation ended?"
- "Did anything you discussed with Max lead you to do something — look something up, talk to someone, apply for anything?"
  - *If yes:* "What made you take that step?"
  - *If no:* "What would have needed to be different for you to do something after?"
- "Was there anything Max surfaced that you thought — 'I'd like to explore this more' — but then didn't? What stopped you?"

*Listen for: whether officers naturally bridge from reflection to action, or whether the conversation just ends.*

---

### Core: CareerCompass awareness + use (8 min)

*This section tests awareness and activation friction independently of Max.*

- "Have you heard of CareerCompass [or OTEP]? What do you know about it?"
  - *If aware:* "Have you used it? Tell me about that."
  - *If not:* "No worries — CareerCompass is a platform we're building for officers to explore STIPs, Gigs, courses, and more. Does that sound like something you'd use?"

**If they have browsed opportunities before (any channel):**
- "Walk me through the last time you looked at a STIP or Gig. What made you look?"
- "Did you end up applying?"
  - *If yes:* "What tipped you over into applying?"
  - *If no:* "What held you back?"
- "Is there a version of engaging with an opportunity that felt lower-stakes — something you'd do before you were ready to apply?"

**Friction probe:**
- "What would make you feel ready to apply for something like a STIP or Gig?"
- "Has there been a moment where you saw an opportunity that looked interesting but you just... didn't act on it? What was going through your head?"

---

### Core: the handoff question (5 min)

*This directly tests the OST branch 1 assumption — the bridge from Max to CareerCompass.*

- "Imagine you just finished a conversation with Max about your career. You've done some reflection and you're feeling clearer. What would you want to happen next?"
- "If Max could send you somewhere — or give you something to take away — what would actually be useful?"
- "Would a link to CareerCompass at the end of a Max session feel helpful or like noise?"
  - Probe: "What would make it feel worth clicking?"
- "What would a 'perfect next step' look like after a coaching conversation?"

*Don't pitch the CTA. Let them describe what they'd want — and listen for whether it matches what we're building.*

---

### Wrap-up (3 min)

- "Is there anything I didn't ask that you think is important for us to understand?"
- "Who else do you think I should talk to — especially anyone who's either really engaged with career development or really not?"
- "Any questions for me?"

> "This has been genuinely useful. We'll use what we learn to shape how the platform works — I'm not going to share anything you've said in a way that identifies you. Thanks for being honest."

## Reminders before each session

- Let them finish. Don't fill silences — count to 5 before speaking.
- Compliments ("That's a great idea!") are noise. Ignore them.
- If they describe a solution, redirect: "What problem were you trying to solve when you thought of that?"
- You're not there to validate Max or CareerCompass. You're there to understand their career development behaviour.
- If they say "I would probably..." ask: "Has that ever actually happened?"
