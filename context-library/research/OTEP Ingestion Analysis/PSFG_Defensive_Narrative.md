# POSITION PAPER: Why PSFG is Deferred from OTEP MVP 1

*June 2026 | For discussion with Diana (Thu 2026-06-19)*

**Bottom line:** PSFG is valuable, but it is not yet competency-linked, platform-managed, or measurable in a consistent way — which would dilute the MVP's ability to test its core hypothesis.

---

## 1. Acknowledging the Intent

PSFG represents an important and growing category of opportunities on the OTG platform. It serves a distinct purpose — encouraging public officers to contribute back to the community, build softer skills, and engage beyond their immediate work. The value of making these opportunities visible to officers is not in dispute.

From 2023 to 2025, PSFG grew from 2 to 11 new opportunities per year, reflecting increasing institutional investment in this space. Host agencies range from Agency for Integrated Care and Ministry of Social and Family Development to National Heritage Board and People's Association. The question is not whether PSFG matters. The question is whether it is ready to be included in a competency-driven MVP — and at this point, the data says it is not.

---

## 2. What the OTEP MVP is Trying to Achieve

The OTEP MVP is scoped to test one specific hypothesis: that officers will take action when they can clearly see their competency gaps and are presented with relevant, competency-linked opportunities.

To test this cleanly, the MVP requires opportunities that are:
- Clearly linked to competency development
- Structured and standardised in how they are presented
- Trackable end-to-end within the platform

STIPs, Gigs, Jobs, and SJRs meet all three conditions. PSFG currently does not.

---

## 3. Why PSFG Does Not Fit Today

An analysis of all 20 PSFG opportunities created between 2023 to 2025 reveals three structural gaps:

**Gap 1: Competency linkage is inconsistent**

- Opportunities with competencies tagged: 0 out of 20 (0%)
- Opportunities showing N/A: 20 out of 20 (100%)

This is a host org compliance gap. Without a competency signal, the platform cannot match officers to PSFG based on development needs — which is the core mechanic the MVP is built around.

**Gap 2: Application flow is not standardised**

- Opportunities with no platform-managed application flow: 20 out of 20 (100%)
- Opportunities with no extractable FormSG link: 10 out of 20 (50%)
- Opportunities using host org's own portals: at least 5 out of 20

Officers are taken off-platform at different points, through different channels, with no standardised experience.

**Gap 3: Outcomes are not captured in the platform**

- Total applicants recorded in OTG across all 20 PSFG opportunities: 0
- Opportunities showing Filled: Yes — 0 out of 20 (0%)
- Opportunities with attendance or feedback data: 0 out of 20 (0%)

These zeros do not mean PSFG has no participation — they mean participation is invisible to the platform.

---

## 4. This is a Sequencing Decision, Not a Rejection

Including PSFG in the MVP in its current state would introduce:
- Inconsistent user journeys (officers taken off-platform through different channels)
- Gaps in tracking and metrics (zero applications, zero outcomes, skewing MVP measurement)
- Ambiguity in opportunity positioning (no competency tags, so officers can't see the development relevance)

The risk is not just incomplete data — it is drawing the wrong conclusions from the pilot because the data is structurally misleading.

---

## 5. What Needs to Happen for PSFG to be Included

| Condition | What's needed |
|---|---|
| Competency tagging | Host orgs to consistently identify OCCs/FCs before posting |
| Standardised application flow | Agree on FormSG or in-platform flow; eliminate ad-hoc portals |
| Participation tracking | Capture sign-up counts via FormSG integration or manual reporting |
| Agency data | Mandate Agency field for all PSFG submissions (currently 30% missing) |
| Product positioning | Align on whether PSFG sits as its own type or under STIP/Gig |

None of these are insurmountable — but they require decisions and process changes on the programme side before the data side can execute.

---

## 6. Recommendation

For OTEP MVP 1, keep scope focused on STIPs, Gigs, Jobs, and SJRs. PSFG is not excluded permanently. It is intentionally deferred to ensure that when it is included, it is set up to succeed.

**Proposed next step:** Agree on the conditions above with the PSFG programme team and set a target milestone for PSFG to be included in a subsequent OTEP release.

---

## 7. Stakeholder Alignment (added 2026-06-16)

Adrian and Xian Zhang are aligned on PSFG exclusion from MVP. Two reasons confirmed in the 2026-06-16 adhoc:
1. Voluntary nature of PSFG means officer intent is materially different from STIPs/Gigs/Jobs.
2. OTG data confirms scarce applications relative to opportunities posted.
