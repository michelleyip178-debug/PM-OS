Open questions — for Business Owner

1
I-013 ConfirmedTime commitment required for Gig/STIP only
Ratified 12 June 2026. TC not required for Job, Secondment, PSFG (formerly agilePSD). Gig/STIP remain required. This closes all "TBC" uncertainty on TC. 5 hard-blocked Gigs/STIPs still need agencies to add TC data.
2
I-015 ConfirmedStart date optional for Job and Secondment
Ratified 12 June 2026. Secondments are standing roles — start date genuinely may not exist. Jobs similarly. Gig/STIP/PSFG remain required (time-bound). Unlocked ~200 Secondments and ~61 Jobs without any agency action.
3
I-014 ConfirmedFunction optional (display-only) for all types
Ratified 12 June 2026. A gig without a function is still a valid opportunity — it won't appear in function-filtered views, which is acceptable. Unlocked 174 gigs. Amber to design the null state for the listing card.
4
No type tagHow should opportunities with no [prefix] in the name be handled?
78 opportunities. Options: hard-block from ingestion, ingest as "Unknown" type, or flag for agency to fix before next run. MSF alone accounts for 37 of these; their GigNames are clearly job titles suggesting [Job] is the correct tag.
5
Stale opportunitiesShould open opportunities with a past end date be ingested, skipped, or auto-delisted?
346 open opportunities have passed their end date — 74 by more than a year. 255 of these have zero applicants. Ingesting them risks surfacing dead listings on OTEP and eroding applicant trust.
6
Misused tagsWhat is the correct type for [Female Only] and eligibility-as-tag entries?
[Female Only] describes eligibility, not opportunity type. [For Grade 12/11 equivalent] and [For MX13-equivalent] are grade filters. All three need a canonical type assigned before ingestion can classify them. A separate eligibility field may be the right solution.
7
Tag normalisationWho owns the canonical tag list, and who is authorised to add to it?
[Innofest] vs [INNOFEST] (duplicate casing) and 18 Job/Secondment sub-variants exist. Without a governed tag taxonomy, new variants will keep appearing and break type classification in each ingestion run.

Risks

2

The creation form cannot be modified. Every new opportunity posted can carry the same missing fields. Without a validation gate at source, the remediation report becomes a recurring manual exercise with each ingestion run.

ESG was 178 of 473 blocked under the old rules (38%). Under v3, ~150 ESG records unlocked via the StartDate rule. ~44 ESG records remain blocked: ~19 TypeTag (unresolvable without DevOps fix) and ~25 other field gaps. ESG is still the single largest blocked agency but less dominant than before.
4

346 open opportunities have passed their end date. Surfacing these on OTEP as live opportunities — especially 74 that expired more than a year ago — risks applicants applying to roles that no longer exist.
5

I-013 confirmed 12 Jun 2026: TC required for Gig/STIP only. PSFG (formerly agilePSD) is TC-exempt. All "TBC" uncertainty on TC is resolved. The 415 passing count is stable. Remaining blocked (218) is not contingent on BO rulings.
6

GigIDs 13733 (Testing 2), 14807, and 14813 are sandbox records with Open status. They will surface in OTEP as live opportunities unless explicitly excluded by the ingestion pipeline.
7
Low7 opportunities have no named owner — no one to contact for remediation
Owned by the shared inbox otg@psd.gov.sg. If these opportunities need remediation, there is no individual accountable to act on the report.

Assumptions baked into the ingestion logic

1
LogicOpportunity type is derived solely from the [prefix] in GigName
No dedicated Type column exists in the source data. The ingestion logic classifies type by regex on the name prefix. Any opportunity without a recognised prefix is treated as Unknown and flagged.
2
I-013 ConfirmedTime commitment required for Gig and STIP only; not required for Secondment, Job, SJR, PSFG
Ratified 12 June 2026. agilePSD is now classified as PSFG (TC not required, consistent with Job/Secondment). All "TBC" uncertainty on TC is resolved.
3
I-013 ConfirmedagilePSD reclassified as PSFG — TC not required
Confirmed 12 June 2026. agilePSD opportunities are now classified under the PSFG category (Public Service–related programme). TC is not required for PSFG. The 5 agilePSD gigs blocked for missing Function remain blocked — agencies must supply Function data.
4
Data"Open" status = intended to be live on OTEP
Some Open opportunities have past end dates or zero applicants, suggesting they may be abandoned. The assumption is that Status alone drives ingestion eligibility unless overridden by a staleness exclusion rule (not yet defined).
5
DataThe 7 identified required fields are the complete ingestion schema
Derived from what is visibly rendered on the OTEP listing card and detail page (LocationName, TimeCommitment, GigStart, GigEnd, Function, BusinessUnit, type tag). If OTEP requires additional backend fields not shown in the UI, the blocked count could be higher.
6
ProcessAgency admins are responsible for fixing their own opportunities using the remediation report
No central team has write access to correct all records. The remediation report (OTEP_Remediation_Report_v2.xlsx) assumes per-agency distribution where each agency fixes its own tab. The source file used for ingestion must be a fresh export at go-live, not the snapshot analysed here.
