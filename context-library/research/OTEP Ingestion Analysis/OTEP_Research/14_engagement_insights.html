The hidden problem: fixing data quality may not fix the catalogue

Even among the 415 opportunities that currently pass all validation rules (v3), the zero-applicant and staleness problems persist. Data completeness is a necessary but not sufficient condition for a healthy OTEP catalogue. Engagement is a separate failure mode.

The "dead catalogue" problem

Stale + zero applicants (all open opportunities)
312
Opportunities that are past their end date AND have attracted no applicants. Ingesting these adds noise, not value. 49% of all open opportunities fall here.
Passing opportunities with zero applicants
~315
Of the 415 opportunities that pass under v3 rules, ~76% are estimated to have 0 applicants (pre-v3: 122 of 160). These pass the pipeline but fail at engagement. Data quality alone didn't fix the problem.
Stale opportunities that still pass validation
59
59 passing opportunities have an end_date in the past. Without a staleness policy (D3 pending), these enter OTEP and show expired opportunities to users.
SJR — engagement paradox
95%
SJR is the cleanest recognised type (only 33% blocked) yet has a 95% zero-applicant rate. Fixing data quality here won't move the needle on engagement.
