Scoring methodology

Dimension 1
Data Quality Score
% of the agency's opportunities that pass all hard field validations. Higher = fewer missing required fields.
Weight: 40%
Dimension 2
Engagement Score
Inverse of zero-applicant rate. Higher = opportunities are attracting talent. Proxy for listing quality and relevance.
Weight: 35%
Dimension 3
Freshness Score
Inverse of staleness rate. Higher = fewer expired listings still marked Open in OTG.
Weight: 25%
