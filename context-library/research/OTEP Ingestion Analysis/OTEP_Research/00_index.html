6 BO decisions — 5 open, 1 confirmed · ordered by priority

D6 How should OTEP handle ringfenced (audience-filtered) opportunities? P1 · Resolve first

38% of open OTG opportunities (253 of 669) have audience filters — visible only to officers from specific agencies. Enterprise Singapore alone holds 169 ringfenced opportunities (67%), mostly internal Secondments and IJPs. OTEP must decide whether to exclude, enforce, or flag these. This decision also affects D5 — 51% of Secondments are ringfenced, so D6 is a prerequisite to resolving Secondment ingestion.

A ★ Enforce ringfence in OTEP: each officer sees only opportunities their agency is eligible for. Best user experience. Requires officer agency data in OTEP session context.
B Carve out ESG (169 internal-only opportunities) and flag the remaining 84 ringfenced opps with a "Restricted" badge. Pragmatic short-term fix.
C Exclude all 253 ringfenced opportunities — add R13 to pipeline. Simplest but loses 38% of the open catalogue.
At stake: 253 opportunities (38% of open catalogue) + OTEP's ability to serve personalised feeds. SJR is ringfence-free (0%) and safe to ingest immediately regardless of this decision.

D5 How do Secondment, agilePSD, and Other (TBC) map to the 5 OTEP categories? P2 · Highest volume impact

OTEP recognises 5 categories only: GIG, STIP, JOB, C@G Jobs, SJR. But 344 open OTG opportunities carry prefixes (Secondment, agilePSD, Other variants) that don't map to any of these categories — they are blocked at classification (R4). This is now the single largest blocker in the pipeline.

Secondment: 259 blocked agilePSD: 29 blocked Other (TBC): 56 blocked
⚠ D6 prerequisite for Secondments: 51% of Secondments (133 of 259) are ringfenced to specific agencies. The routing decision for Secondments cannot be finalised until D6 (ringfencing policy) is resolved. D6 should be discussed before D5.
A ★ Map to nearest OTEP category — e.g. Secondment→JOB, agilePSD→GIG or SJR. Confirm exact mapping, update R4 classifier, re-run pipeline. No platform schema change needed.
B Exclude Secondment, agilePSD, and Other from OTEP scope entirely. Only the 5 canonical categories are ingested. Simplest long-term, but 344 opportunities permanently out of scope.
C Create new OTEP categories for Secondment and/or agilePSD. Unlocks all 344 but requires OTEP platform schema change and re-classification logic.
At stake: 344 opportunities (54% of all currently blocked). This decision unblocks more opportunities than any other single action remaining. Note: JOB vs C@G Jobs split within the existing 129 "Job" opportunities also needs data verification.

D3 Stale opportunity policy P3 · Open

346 open opportunities have passed their end date — 74 by more than a year. 255 of these have zero applicants. Should these appear on OTEP?

A ★ Exclude stale opportunities with zero applicants (255). Live stale opportunities with applicants (91) still pass — someone is interested.
B Exclude all stale opportunities (346). Cleanest catalogue but removes some active-interest listings.
C Ingest all as-is. Applicants may apply to roles that no longer exist.
At stake: Applicant trust. Stale listings with no owner erode confidence in the platform.
⚠ Baseline note: The current 12 ingestion rules do not include a staleness filter. Some of the 160 opportunities passing today may have past end dates. D3 would introduce this as a new filter — meaning the baseline of 160 could decrease slightly depending on which option is chosen.

D4 Opportunities with no type tag P4 · Open

78 opportunities have no [type prefix] in their name. Without a type, OTEP cannot classify or validate them. Most appear to be Jobs from MSF and Enterprise Singapore.

A ★ Hard block — route to remediation report. Agencies must add a prefix before next ingestion run. Simple and enforceable.
B Ingest as "Uncategorised" — visible on OTEP but not type-filtered. Risks permanent orphan listings.
At stake: Tag discipline going forward. Without enforcement, new untyped opportunities will appear every ingestion run.

D2 Start & end date exemptions for Secondments, Jobs, SJRs ✓ Confirmed

Confirmed 10 June 2026: Start date and end date are not mandatory for Secondment, Job, and SJR types. These are standing or evergreen roles where a fixed start/end date may not apply.

✓ Decision Secondment, Job, and SJR are exempt from start date and end date requirements. Up to ~261 opportunities unblocked on these fields — the largest single unlock in the pipeline.
Impact: Secondment (200), Job (61) no longer blocked on start date; Secondment (38) no longer blocked on end date. Exemption applied at type level — no schema change required.
