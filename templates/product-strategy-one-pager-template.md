# Product Strategy One-Pager Template

- One-pagers are a means for you to declare what you do or what you believe.
- It is the core artefact PMs are expected to use to describe the product's problem and solution.
- It should be written in the style to a reader/stakeholder that has no knowledge or context of the problem space.
- *[Applicable only for PM conversions]* If you are writing in the context of a product/project that already exists today, do not be constrained by what has already been done and decided. If you had to formulate this strategy again, how would you do it whilst applying the concepts below?

## 3. Strategy

### 3.1 Proposed Product Strategy

*Describe the longer-term strategy (that leads from the product vision) and outline the solutions to address the problem.*
- What are the hypotheses that will be tested to validate the strategy?
- Theory of change: How will the proposed strategy address the problem?
- What are the product's guiding principles?
- How will the product advance the agency's policy and business objectives?

### 3.2 Risks and Mitigations

*What risks have you identified with your approach?*
- Address at least three types of risks: market, technical, and team.
- What experiments (e.g. pilot/POC/POV) would be proposed to mitigate the product's key risks?
- If mitigations were already conducted, what were the outcomes of these experiments?
- What quantitative metrics and data prove that key risks were sufficiently mitigated?
