## Steps

### Part 1 — Demo Prep

#### Step 1 — Identify what's demo-able
From 00-hub/sprint-status.md, list stories that were completed
this sprint and are worth showing. A story is demo-able if:
- It produces a visible user-facing change
- It's a meaningful step toward the sprint goal
- It can be shown in under 2 minutes

#### Step 2 — Suggest demo order
Order stories for maximum narrative impact — not by story ID.
Lead with the most tangible outcome for the user.

#### Step 3 — Prepare the demo narrative
For each demo item, write a 1-sentence setup:
"This sprint, we wanted to [goal]. Here's what we built."

---

### Part 2 — Retro Prep (Signal → Sense → Shift)

#### Step 4 — Signal: What happened this sprint?
Prompt Michelle to reflect on:
- What went well? (ship it again)
- What was harder than expected?
- What surprised us?
- Where did we lose time?

Generate 3-5 starter signals based on the sprint context.

#### Step 5 — Sense: What does it mean?
For each signal, ask: is this a one-off or a pattern?
Help Michelle distinguish noise from something worth addressing.

#### Step 6 — Shift: What will we do differently?
For the 1-2 most important patterns, suggest a concrete action item
with a clear owner and a test for whether it worked next sprint.
